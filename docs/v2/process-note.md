**Consumer-First: a brand motion study**

What does "consumer-first" look like when it moves? In this film you follow one person through a mortgage: a wall of percentage boxes between them and home, a crowd quietly losing money, a fall into a system that won't compete. Then the brand arrives as a flood that starts _from them_. Rates compete around the person, the winner becomes the document in their hand, and the obstacles from the opening flatten into the line they walk home on.

The film rests on a small motion system that someone else could pick up:

- four easing curves, each with one job;
- a duration scale and a reading-time rule;
- a rigged character that anticipates, acts and settles;
- five named transitions;
- sound edited to the same timeline as the picture.

Created during a final-round process for a Senior Product Designer, Brand role. v1 was a 2-hour sprint directing GPT-6 Astra in Codex; v2 is a directed revision built around a brand motion system, executed with Claude.
